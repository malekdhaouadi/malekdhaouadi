## About

I am completing a double degree: an MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at **DSTI School of Engineering** in Paris, alongside the **engineering diploma in Financial Informatics & Engineering (INFINI)** from **ESPRIT**, Tunisia, accredited by EUR-ACE and CTI.
